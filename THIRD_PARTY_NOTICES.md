## AFK Shipwrecks

Salvage Helper's code is its own, but many of its features were modelled on AFK Shipwrecks
(https://github.com/Brettgod1355/AFK-Shipwrecks), and some game facts were taken from it, which it
verified in game. Those facts are: cargo hold capacities by tier, shipwreck Sailing levels and
lifetimes, the 8-tile hook reach, crew job varbit values, the varbits for boat slot, boat HP and
poison, the chat and overhead phrases, the 0x7FFF container id mask, the grouping of hook
animations into working, idle and sorting, the list of items the cargo hold accepts, and reading
crewmates from the game's crew table.
